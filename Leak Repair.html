Know The Warning Signs

Do You Have a Hidden Plumbing Leak?

Many dangerous plumbing leaks occur behind walls or under concrete foundations. Watch out for these common warning signs in your home or business.

Spiking Water Bills

An unexpected increase in your monthly water utility bill without a change in usage is often the first indicator of a silent underground or concealed pipe leak.

Wall & Ceiling Stains

Yellowish or brown water rings, bubbling paint, or warping drywall on ceilings and walls point directly to a leaking supply line or drain pipe behind the surface.

Drop in Water Pressure

If your shower or faucets suddenly lose pressure when you turn them on, water may be escaping through a crack or burst joint somewhere along your plumbing line.

Warm Spots on Floors

Warm or damp areas on your tile, laminate, or carpet flooring often signal a hot water line slab leak beneath your home's concrete foundation.

Musty Odors & Mold

Persistent damp smells or visible black mildew around baseboards, cabinets, or bathroom corners thrive on moisture from slow, undetected drips.

Sound of Running Water

Hearing a faint hissing, rushing, or dripping sound inside your walls when all faucets and appliances are turned off is a clear sign of an active pipe leak.
