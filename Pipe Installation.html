Spot The Problem Early

4 Signs Your Home's Pipes Are Failing

If your home was built more than 25 years ago, watch out for these symptoms of internal pipe decay.

Plummeting Water Pressure

Rust buildup inside old galvanized pipes chokes water flow down to a trickle when multiple taps are running.

Rusty Morning Water

Brown or orange-tinted tap water in the morning indicates interior iron pipe flaking into your drinking supply.

Recurring Pinhole Leaks

Fixing one leak only for another to pop up soon after means your entire pipe network has reached the end of its life.

Polybutylene Pipes

Installed between 1978 and 1995, gray polybutylene pipes react with chlorine and rupture without warning.
