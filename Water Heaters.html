Diagnostics

Warning Signs Your Heater Needs Service

Recognize these common indicators before a total breakdown occurs.

Lukewarm Water

Water turns cold quickly or fails to heat properly due to a failing thermostat or heating element.

Fixed in under 1 hour

Rumbling Noises

Loud popping or banging sounds indicate heavy sediment buildup hardening at the tank bottom.

Pro Tank Flushing

Rusty Discoloration

Brownish hot water points to internal corrosion and a degraded sacrificial anode rod.

Anode Replacement

Pooling Moisture

Water puddling around the base indicates tank fracture, valve failure, or loose fittings.
