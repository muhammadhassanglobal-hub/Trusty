Why Store-Bought Liquid Cleaners Do More Harm Than Good

Before pouring a bottle of harsh acid down your sink, see how it compares to a professional TrustyPlumb drain cleaning.

The Risky Way

Store-Bought Chemical Cleaners

  • Melts & Warps PVC Pipes: Caustic chemical reactions generate intense heat that softens plastic pipes and ruins glue joints.
  • Eats Away Old Metal: Sulfuric acid corrodes cast-iron and copper traps, leading to expensive hidden leaks under your cabinet.
  • Temporary Tunnel Effect: Liquids only burn a tiny hole through the clog—leaving 85% of the sludge behind to block up again days later.
  • Toxic Fumes & Splash Hazard: Dangerous for children, pets, and septic tank bacteria.
The TrustyPlumb Way

Professional Mechanical & Hydro Clearing

  • 100% Pipe-Safe: Uses controlled mechanical cables and pure pressurized water tailored to your pipe's exact age and material.
  • Full-Diameter Cleaning: Restores 100% of the pipe's original flow capacity from wall to wall.
  • Reaches Deep Sewer Lines: Clears blockages 100+ feet underground where liquid cleaners get diluted and useless.
  • Backed by Our Flow Guarantee: Peace of mind knowing the root cause was verified on camera.

Drain Cleaning FAQs

If only one sink or shower is draining slowly, the clog is isolated in that fixture's branch pipe. However, if flushing a toilet causes water to gurgle or back up into your bathtub, or multiple downstairs fixtures clog at once, your main sewer line is blocked.
We always perform a camera inspection first! For fragile or aging pipes, our technicians dial down the PSI pressure or use specialized descaling chains and rotary augers so your pipes are cleaned safely without structural stress.
That sulfur smell is usually caused by decaying food, bacteria, and biofilm coating the walls of the drain pipe or overflow tube—or a dry P-trap allowing sewer gas to enter. A thorough enzymatic and mechanical drain cleaning eliminates the odor immediately.
For most households, a preventative main line inspection and kitchen drain cleaning every 18 to 24 months keeps grease and tree roots from ever causing a surprise emergency backup.
