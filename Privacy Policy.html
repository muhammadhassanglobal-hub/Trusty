Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how TrustyPlumb collects, uses, and protects your personal information with complete transparency.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to TrustyPlumb ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our residential and commercial plumbing services.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when scheduling a plumbing service, requesting a quote, or contacting us via phone or email. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Your full name, service address, billing address, email address, and phone number.
  • Service Details: Information about your property, plumbing issues, service history, and preferred appointment times.
  • Payment Data: Billing details and payment information required to process transactions securely.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website interaction data collected automatically via cookies.

3 How We Use Your Information

TrustyPlumb uses the collected data for the following business and service purposes:

  • To dispatch licensed plumbers and provide residential or 24/7 emergency plumbing services.
  • To provide accurate upfront estimates, invoices, and service receipts.
  • To communicate with you regarding your appointment, service updates, or customer support inquiries.
  • To improve our website functionality, customer service experience, and service offerings.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and warranties.

4 Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We respect your privacy and never sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Technicians & Partners: With our certified field technicians and trusted subcontractors strictly to fulfill your plumbing service request.
  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who assist us with payment processing, scheduling software, or website hosting under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TrustyPlumb and our customers.

5 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use "cookies" and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors are coming from. You can choose to set your web browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect how certain parts of our website function.

6 Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location across the United States, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal or record-keeping exceptions.
  • Opt-out of receiving promotional communications or marketing emails at any time.

8 Children's Privacy

Our website and plumbing services are intended for adult homeowners and business operators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take steps to delete it immediately.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

TrustyPlumb reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
